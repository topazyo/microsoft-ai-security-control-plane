## Validate before you rely on anything here

- **Statuses change.** Microsoft AI-security capabilities move between Roadmap, Public Preview, and Generally Available continuously — sometimes with regional or licensing differences. Every row in this repository carries a **last-verified date**; treat any row older than the current monthly refresh window as potentially stale.
- **Verify in your own environment.** Release status in documentation does not guarantee availability or behavior in your tenant. Confirm each capability's status, licensing, and actual behavior in your own tenant before making architecture, compliance, or leadership commitments based on it.
- **Contested statuses are labelled, not guessed.** Where Microsoft's own documentation conflicts, rows are marked **Requires further validation** rather than resolved by assumption.
